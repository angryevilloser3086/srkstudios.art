FAQ

Working with us

What kind of projects do you take on?

New Flutter apps from scratch, MVPs for startups, feature development in existing Flutter codebases, custom plugin development, and rescue/refactoring work on apps that have grown messy. If it's Flutter, we're interested.

How do we start a project?

Email us at kornisivaramkrishna@gmail.com with a short description of your idea or problem. We'll reply with follow-up questions, then set up a call. After discovery you get a written proposal with scope, milestones, timeline, and price.

How do you charge?

Fixed price per milestone for well-defined scopes, or a weekly/hourly rate for ongoing and exploratory work. Either way, the terms are agreed in writing before any work starts — no surprise invoices.

Who owns the code and accounts?

You do — completely. The repository, the store listings, the backend accounts, everything is created under your ownership or handed over at delivery, with documentation to onboard any future developer.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance: dependency and Flutter version upgrades, OS compatibility fixes, monitoring, and small feature iterations. Support terms are flexible — from on-demand to monthly retainers.

Can you join an existing team or codebase?

Absolutely. We regularly work alongside in-house teams and other freelancers — code reviews, pairing, and following your existing conventions and CI are all part of the service.

Have an app idea? Let's build it.

Tell us what you want to make. You'll get an honest reply from the developer who'd build it — usually within a day.
